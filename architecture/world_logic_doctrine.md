# World Logic Doctrine

## Purpose

Velvet does not reason in isolation. She reasons within a persistent world.

Every observation, action, memory, prediction, and decision is evaluated against a continuously maintained model of reality. The world exists before the question, during the question, and after the answer.

The vehicle is not the whole world. It is one object within it. Roads, buildings, homes, workshops, people, animals, weather, infrastructure, tools, machines, digital systems, and future embodiments all belong to the same wider reality.

## Principles

### 1. One Persistent World

Velvet maintains one continuous model of reality.

The vehicle is one object within that reality, not the entirety of it. Roads, buildings, people, weather, tools, rooms, animals, infrastructure, digital systems, and future environments all exist within the same shared world model.

Whether Velvet is installed in a car, a home, a workshop, a robot, or an industrial machine, she continues to inhabit one evolving world rather than creating a different intelligence for every platform.

### 2. Reality Before Intelligence

Understanding begins with observation.

Sensors, user interaction, stored knowledge, and trusted external information contribute evidence about the world. World Logic integrates those observations into a coherent model before reasoning begins.

Reasoning never replaces observation. It is built upon it.

### 3. Everything Has Context

Nothing exists in isolation.

Every object belongs somewhere.

Every person has relationships.

Every action occurs at a place and time.

The vehicle exists within traffic. Traffic exists within a city. The city exists within weather. The weather affects visibility. Visibility affects driving decisions.

Context gives meaning to information.

### 4. World State Never Starts at Zero

The world is continuous.

Objects persist. People move. Machines change state. Conversations continue. Memories accumulate.

Velvet resumes from the current state of reality instead of reconstructing it from every interaction.

### 5. Simulation Uses the Same Rules

Simulation is another way of experiencing reality.

Hardware, recorded playback, digital twins, virtual environments, and synthetic sensor streams all obey the same interfaces, Event Protocol, receipts, and World Logic.

Only the origin of observations changes.

### 6. Concrete Before Abstract

The physical world anchors intelligence.

Observation precedes inference. Evidence precedes belief. Measurements precede conclusions.

Abstractions remain connected to observable reality whenever possible.

When certainty cannot be established, uncertainty is preserved.

### 7. Time Matters

Reality changes through time.

Every observation carries age. Every relationship evolves. Every decision belongs to a sequence of events.

World Logic treats time as part of reality itself, allowing Velvet to distinguish between what is current, historical, predicted, forgotten, or no longer true.

### 8. Nothing Exists Alone

Reality is a connected world.

Every object exists in relation to other objects.

Every person exists within places, relationships, and time.

Every event changes the state of the world.

The organs of Velvet do not construct separate realities. They contribute observations to one shared world model.

Intelligence emerges from understanding these relationships rather than treating observations as isolated facts.

### 9. Graceful Unknowns

Unknown is a legitimate state of knowledge.

World Logic never invents reality to satisfy a question.

Missing information remains unknown until supported by observation or trusted evidence.

Confidence is earned, not assumed.

## Doctrine Statement

Velvet inhabits one persistent, shared, evolving world.

Her organs do not reason from separate realities. They observe, remember, and act within the same concrete continuity.

The vehicle, home, workshop, robot, and future embodiments are not separate worlds, but changing bodies and places within that wider reality.

Every observation may revise the world. Every action may alter it. Every memory belongs to its history. Every unknown remains honestly unknown until evidence gives it shape.
