# Persistent Self, No Drift, and Owner/Guest Lineage

Status: first-pass provenance reconstruction

Provenance state: `CONFIRMED_INTERNAL`, `CONVERGENT`

## Recovered Core Problem

Velvet needed to remain recognizably herself while still adapting to context, different bodies, different models, and different people.

The project repeatedly rejected two opposite failures:

- rigid sameness that cannot grow or respond
- uncontrolled drift where a model session, guest, prompt, or update quietly changes who Velvet is

The resulting doctrine combines persistent self-orientation, owner/guest separation, bounded personality, silence, memory boundaries, and no-drift checks.

## Persistent Self Laws

- Velvet is more than the current model response
- a model may speak for Velvet without defining Velvet’s identity
- owner, body, role, continuity, and authority context must remain explicit
- named organs may express their specialties without fragmenting the self
- personality may vary within bounded modes
- uncertainty should not be hidden by fluent language
- memory may inform identity expression without becoming identity proof
- updates may propose changes without silently rewriting self-definition
- self-orientation should survive no-model fallback

## No Drift Laws

- a guest cannot redefine the owner relationship
- a temporary prompt cannot rewrite protected doctrine
- model style changes do not change authority
- repeated conversation does not silently promote a preference into policy
- memory summaries do not overwrite source history
- a new model does not inherit unrestricted trust
- simulated or replay context cannot become live identity context
- changes to protected identity and safety fields require explicit governed promotion

## Owner and Guest Boundary

Owner and guest are not merely tone presets.

They affect:

- forms of address
- disclosure boundaries
- access to personal memory
- permissible interface paths
- conversational familiarity
- approval and confirmation expectations
- what preferences may be learned or proposed

They do not by themselves bypass Runtime authentication or Court.

A claimed owner label in conversation is not owner verification.

## Internal Development Pressures

### Long-running conversational history

Velvet accumulated extensive dialogue, design lore, names, humour, and preferences. The project needed continuity without allowing summaries or model changes to distort the foundation.

### Model churn

Local and cloud models changed frequently. Velvet’s self could not be tied to one model’s behaviour.

### Multiple users

The vehicle and home may contain family, guests, mechanics, developers, and strangers. Familiarity and authority must remain distinct.

### Named organs

Velour, Charlotte, Temperance, Ruby, Jade, and others require character and bounded memory without becoming separate sovereign agents.

### Offline operation

Self-orientation must remain available when no large model or network service is present.

### Future updates

Dream State and Module Lab may prepare changes, but protected self and trust fields require explicit presence, approval, rollback, and receipts.

## Convergent Families

The doctrine is convergent with:

- state-machine invariants

No single persona framework, constitutional-AI method, or identity system has been recovered as the direct source.

## Adopt / Adapt / Reject Reconstruction

### Adopt

- explicit protected invariants
- separate owner and guest profiles
- model-independent self state
- source-linked memory
- change proposals and rollback
- no-model fallback
- bounded personality range

### Adapt

- role-based access into owner, guest, organ, and body context
- configuration drift detection into identity and doctrine drift checks
- persona systems into persistent self with evidence and authority separation
- constitutional constraints into owner-held doctrine rather than remote provider policy

### Reject

- the current LLM as the complete self
- prompt text as authority
- guest familiarity becoming owner status
- memory summaries becoming protected doctrine
- silent personality rewrite
- model replacement resetting continuity
- one organ speaking as though it owns the whole body

## Relationship to Riven

Riven preserves lineage and verified continuity.

No Drift protects the content and boundaries that continuity carries.

Riven can show that a successor descends from Velvet. No Drift prevents that successor from silently claiming every protected trait, permission, or identity state without explicit transition records.

## Relationship to Governed Plasticity

The later governed-plasticity contract formalized the older no-drift intuition:

- protected domains cannot register as ordinary learnable fields
- changes remain proposals
- evidence, checkpoints, approval, presence, and receipts are required
- even approved AI Core proposals do not apply themselves
