# World Logic and Identity Logic Lineage

Status: first-pass provenance reconstruction

Provenance state: `CONFIRMED_INTERNAL`, `CONVERGENT`

## Recovered Core Problem

Velvet’s organs needed to share more than messages.

They needed a common, persistent, uncertainty-aware account of:

- what entities exist
- where they are
- what body and installation are active
- what changed
- when it changed
- which evidence supports the claim
- whether the evidence is simulated, stale, disputed, or incomplete
- which identity claims remain stable through movement, repair, duplication, and succession

World Logic and Identity Logic were written to prevent every organ from inventing a private reality.

## World Logic Laws

- there is one shared world, not one private world per organ
- observations are not automatically facts
- time, location, source, confidence, and freshness travel with claims
- simulation and live evidence use compatible structures but remain distinguishable
- contradictions remain visible
- missing evidence remains missing
- stale state is not silently refreshed by narrative
- an organ may contribute evidence without owning the whole world model
- world state does not grant authority

## Identity Logic Laws

- identity is not a label
- identity is not role
- identity is not authority
- aliases do not create new entities
- recognition evidence may be possible, likely, corroborated, disputed, or rejected
- succession preserves lineage without pretending sameness
- duplicates remain distinct until evidence proves otherwise
- changing body, position, module, or name does not automatically change identity
- uncertainty must survive presentation
- no observation may self-promote into verified identity

## Internal Development Pressures

### Multi-organ perception

Camera, microphone, NFC, seat, GNSS, CAN, and other organs may observe the same person, vehicle, location, or event differently.

### Multiple embodiments

Velvet may inhabit the Tiburon, Western Star, house, forge, simulated body, or future successor installation.

### Recognition safety

A face match, voice match, seat presence, or device signal is evidence. It is not owner authority by itself.

### Time and stale state

Moving vehicles and distributed nodes produce delayed, reordered, and clock-dependent observations.

### Simulation

Fake and live bodies must share the same world grammar without simulated identity leaking into physical access.

### Continuity

Riven required a world model that could distinguish current body, remembered body, predecessor, successor, and uncertain recovery.

## Adopt / Adapt / Reject Reconstruction

### Adopt

- stable entity identifiers
- temporal and spatial relations
- confidence and evidence links
- explicit lifecycle states
- contradiction and dispute records
- source and receipt references
- live, fixture, and replay posture
- lineage relationships

### Adapt

- robotics world models into a shared Unified-Organ reality
- knowledge graphs into evidence-bounded body and relationship state
- identity resolution into owner-safe multimodal recognition
- digital-twin state into declared simulation with no authority leakage
- temporal databases into monotonic, freshness, and continuity-aware transitions

### Reject

- model narrative as canonical world state
- one sensor declaring identity
- automatic merge of similar entities
- silent repair of contradictions
- simulation creating physical credentials
- role names being treated as identity proof
- world-model confidence becoming Court permission

## Relationship to Cognitive Event Layer

World Logic answers:

> What appears to exist and be true across time and space?

The Cognitive Event Layer answers:

> What appears to be happening now, what is expected next, and where does the event boundary lie?

A cognitive event references world entities and observations. It does not replace them.

## Relationship to Riven

Identity Logic handles entities and relationships within the world model.

Riven handles Velvet’s verified continuity lineage.

A world-model identity record cannot create or replace Riven continuity.

## Relationship to Recognition

Recognition evidence may include:

- image
- voice
- NFC or other trusted local credential
- seat and body position
- touch
- presence and living motion
- device proximity

Corroboration improves an identity claim. Runtime still owns verified session and access context.
