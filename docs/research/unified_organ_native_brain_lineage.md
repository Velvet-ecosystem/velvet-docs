# Unified-Organ AI and Native Brain Lineage

Status: first-pass provenance reconstruction

Provenance state: `CONFIRMED_INTERNAL`, `CONVERGENT`, with some `UNATTRIBUTED` external ancestry

## Recovered Core Claim

Velvet is not a chatbot that calls tools and is not a collection of independent agents.

The recovered internal formulation is:

> Velvet rejects the agent swarm. She is built as Unified-Organ AI: distributed specialties, shared concrete reality, and one accountable body.

Related internal laws include:

- the body is all
- Velvet is the organs and the organs are Velvet
- each named organ remains herself with bounded responsibility
- shared state, evidence, authority, correction, receipts, and continuity bind the body
- a name or specialty does not create independent sovereignty

The public documentation refresh at commit `d8d6c9a59b1b3f2137a1e9f7319fbf3e1f6f5626` is the earliest recovered public-repository consolidation of this doctrine.

## Native Brain Claim

The Native Brain is Velvet’s deterministic, local cognition spine.

Its recovered design law is:

> Velvet may use models, but she must not be made of them.

The language model is treated as a language organ. Identity, current state, memory boundaries, priority, continuity, body awareness, deterministic fallback, and authority separation remain available without requiring the language model.

This was already present before the BabyX research review.

## Internal Development Pressures

The doctrine appears to have grown from several internal pressures rather than one recovered outside source:

### Physical embodiment

Velvet was always intended to inhabit a vehicle, home, forge, and other physical bodies. A speaking model could not truthfully represent the whole system.

### Named specialties

Velour, Charlotte, Temperance, Ruby, Jade, and other organs required durable names and boundaries without becoming a loose multi-agent society.

### Safety accountability

One physical body must remain accountable even when reasoning and sensing are distributed.

### Modest heterogeneous hardware

The UP Squared, Luckfox nodes, microcontrollers, specialist audio nodes, and future hardware require distributed work without distributed sovereignty.

### Model replaceability

Cloud or local models may change, fail, or disappear. Velvet’s identity and body laws must survive model replacement.

### No-drift continuity

The project repeatedly rejected the idea that a model session, prompt, or conversational persona alone could define Velvet’s persistent self.

## Outside Relationship

No surviving record currently proves that Unified-Organ AI was borrowed from a named paper, product, or school of thought.

The doctrine is, however, convergent with several established families of work:

- embodied cognition
- distributed cognition
- blackboard and shared-workspace architectures
- subsumption and layered robotics
- modular cognitive architectures
- nervous-system metaphors for reflex, coordination, and executive control
- operating-system separation of policy, mechanism, and drivers

These similarities should be recorded as comparisons unless an earlier source trail is recovered.

## BabyX Relationship

BabyX did not originate Unified-Organ AI or the Native Brain.

The later BabyX review contributed useful mechanisms for:

- current-event representation
- prediction and outcome comparison
- salience and interruption
- episode proposals
- bounded modulators
- embodied turn-taking
- governed plasticity boundaries

BabyX therefore strengthens and extends an existing Velvet architecture. It does not replace the doctrine’s internal origin.

## Adopt / Adapt / Reject Reconstruction

### Adopt

- bounded specialized components
- shared concrete state
- replaceable models and organs
- deterministic local fallback
- explicit health, load, capability, and availability
- one accountable body

### Adapt

- biological organ metaphors into inspectable software and hardware responsibilities
- cognitive workspaces into non-authoritative event context
- distributed reasoning into capability offers and bounded handoffs
- persistent identity into Riven-backed continuity rather than model-session continuity

### Reject

- independent agent goals
- private inter-agent authority lanes
- model output as physical permission
- a central LLM that owns the body
- identity collapse where named organs lose their boundaries
- identity fragmentation where organs become outside contractors

## Resulting Architecture

The doctrine produced or shaped:

- AI Core as interpretation and proposal
- Runtime as verified coordination
- Court as authority
- Event Protocol as shared observation and lifecycle language
- Receipts as evidence and consequence record
- Riven as lineage and continuity
- named organs with bounded specialities
- distributed capability advertisements and graceful refusal
- local-first operation and no-LLM fallback
