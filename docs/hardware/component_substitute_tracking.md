# Component Substitute Tracking

Chosen parts need documented substitutes before supply becomes urgent.

Track primary and acceptable substitute parts for CAN transceivers and controllers, level shifters, regulators, buck converters, MEMS sensors, motor-control components, opto-isolators, relays, connectors, cameras, GNSS modules, and microphones.

Each record includes electrical, pin, software, and mechanical compatibility; driver impact; required validation; known suppliers; stock posture; and last checked date.

A substitute is not approved because its listing says “compatible.” Promotion requires the tests named in the record, including power envelope, bus behavior, degraded operation, and hardware receipt where relevant. Unknown provenance or silent silicon revision changes remain explicit risks.
