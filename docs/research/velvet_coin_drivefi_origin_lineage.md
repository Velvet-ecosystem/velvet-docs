# Velvet Coin and Drive-Fi Origin Lineage

Status: founder-confirmed foundation reconstruction

Provenance state: `CONFIRMED_INTERNAL`, `FOUNDER_CONFIRMED_ORIGIN`, `CONFIRMED_ARCHIVE_REFERENCE`

## Purpose

This record preserves the place of Velvet Coin and Drive-Fi in Velvet's history without treating the coin as a current runtime dependency, announcing a relaunch, promising utility, or making claims of guaranteed financial value.

The coin was not a decorative token added after Velvet existed.

It was an early foundation branch that forced the project to ask how ownership, participation, attention, safety, evidence, rewards, wallets, and accountable value could exist inside a locally owned physical system.

## Current Status

The founder confirms the following present posture:

- Velvet Coin remains live outside Velvet's operational architecture.
- It may retain potential future utility or value.
- No present Velvet capability, authority path, safety function, receipt path, or physical-control path depends on the coin.
- No current reward mechanism is active inside Velvet.
- Integration remains deliberately deferred.
- A future integration decision would require separate technical, safety, governance, security, legal, and economic review.

Potential value is not guaranteed value.

A live coin is not evidence of approved Velvet integration.

## Why the Coin Existed

The early concept joined two ideas:

1. Velvet should remain owned by the people who build and inhabit her bodies rather than being captured by an OEM or cloud platform.
2. Useful, safe, attentive participation in a physical ecosystem could carry recognized value.

The surviving public language called this branch **Drive-Fi** and described value as emerging from participation rather than passive speculation.

The deeper founder-confirmed reasoning included:

- reward should be connected to meaningful participation
- dangerous driving should reduce or remove reward
- lack of attention should reduce or remove reward
- unsafe behaviour should never become more profitable than safe behaviour
- the system should be able to explain why value was earned, reduced, or withheld
- local evidence and governance should matter more than opaque remote scoring

The exact early scoring rules, thresholds, and implementation sketches have not yet been recovered. Those details remain archive-recovery targets rather than established present doctrine.

## No Geofence Doctrine

Velvet was never intended to restrict where the owner could drive through an OEM-style geofence.

The coin's safety reasoning concerned whether behaviour qualified for reward, not whether software granted permission to enter or leave a geographic area.

The correct distinction is:

```text
owner chooses where to travel
        |
        v
Velvet observes declared safety and participation evidence
        |
        v
reward may be earned, reduced, withheld, or left unresolved
```

Not:

```text
approved geographic zone
        |
        v
software permits travel or participation
```

A reward system may evaluate evidence without becoming a digital fence around the owner.

## Why Integration Was Held Back

The coin and wallet concepts appeared before Velvet had enough trustworthy architecture for value to rest on.

Earlier coin-related material was deliberately sanitized, quarantined, or removed from public technical releases while the project built stronger foundations.

The missing floor included:

- stable identity
- owner and guest separation
- local-first operation
- trustworthy body and vehicle state
- attention and presence evidence
- bounded authority
- Court and capability grants
- execution separation
- canonical receipts
- continuity and lineage
- module trust and package provenance
- sensor confidence and uncertainty
- replay resistance
- anti-gaming rules
- privacy boundaries
- recovery and dispute evidence

The decision was not to abandon the coin.

The decision was to prevent value logic from outrunning identity, safety, evidence, and accountability.

## Sanitization and Dormancy

Archive audits show that earlier public-release work removed or sanitized wallet governance material involving Guardian, Librarian, and Court concepts.

That sanitization should be understood as a boundary decision:

- financial and governance concepts were not ready for public technical integration
- narrative and private governance material had to be separated from reusable public primitives
- wallet and ledger ideas were preserved indirectly through receipts, authority boundaries, and continuity work
- future value integration was deferred until the underlying architecture became more truthful

The historical posture is therefore:

- **foundational** in origin
- **live** outside current operational integration
- **dormant** inside Velvet architecture
- **deferred**, not abandoned

## Foundational Questions Exposed by the Coin

### Participation

What activity deserves recognition?

Motion alone is insufficient. Distance, speed, or time cannot automatically prove useful participation.

### Attention

How does the system distinguish engaged operation from distraction, impairment, exhaustion, absence, or unreliable evidence?

This question helped create pressure for driver-attention, presence, and uncertainty handling.

### Safety

How can a reward system avoid paying more for aggressive, reckless, exhausted, manipulated, or otherwise dangerous behaviour?

A safe system must allow reward reduction, denial, or unresolved status when evidence is poor.

### Evidence

What proves that a reward was deserved?

This question points toward receipts, provenance, sensor confidence, timestamps, source identity, and tamper-evident records.

### Governance

Who defines reward rules, changes them, disputes them, or refuses them?

This question created early pressure for Guardian, Librarian, Court, wallet governance, and owner-visible policy.

### Ownership

Can value remain locally meaningful without giving a cloud platform or OEM silent control over the body, data, wallet, or driver?

This question aligns with Velvet's later local-first, retrofit, and owner-sovereignty doctrines.

### Anti-gaming

How does the system avoid rewarding falsified motion, replayed events, sensor spoofing, unsafe mileage, or manufactured activity?

This question aligns with later nonce protection, package trust, receipts, qualification gates, and adversarial testing.

## Relationship to Later Velvet Architecture

The coin should not be credited as the sole origin of later systems.

It did, however, expose problems that later architecture also had to solve:

```text
participation
  -> evidence
  -> identity
  -> safety and attention
  -> declared policy
  -> bounded decision
  -> receipt
  -> continuity
  -> possible value
```

This is a lineage of design pressure, not a claim that present Runtime, Court, Receipts, Riven, or sensor systems were built directly from token code.

## Adopt / Adapt / Reject Reconstruction

### Adopt

- user ownership matters
- useful physical participation may carry value
- safety and attention should affect reward eligibility
- value decisions require inspectable evidence
- local systems should not depend on opaque cloud scoring
- rewards must not grant physical authority
- governance and dispute paths must be explicit

### Adapt

- movement-based minting into evidence-backed participation
- simple mileage rewards into multi-signal qualification
- wallet history into canonical receipts and continuity pointers
- early Guardian, Librarian, and Court concepts into bounded roles and authority layers
- token activity into a future optional economic surface rather than a core identity requirement
- promotional Drive-Fi language into technical doctrine with uncertainty and safety boundaries

### Reject or Quarantine

- guaranteed value claims
- speculative urgency
- rewards that encourage more driving regardless of context
- incentives that make dangerous behaviour profitable
- hidden scoring
- geofencing as owner control
- cloud custody as a requirement for identity or operation
- token ownership as authority over Velvet's body
- automatic integration merely because the coin remains live
- financial logic inside safety-critical execution paths
