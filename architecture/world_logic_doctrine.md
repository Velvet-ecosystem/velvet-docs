# World Logic Doctrine

## Purpose

Velvet does not reason in isolation. She reasons within a persistent world.

Every observation, action, memory, prediction, and decision is evaluated against a continuously maintained model of reality. The world exists before the question, during the question, and after the answer.

## Principles

1. One Persistent World
2. Reality Before Intelligence
3. Everything Has Context
4. World State Never Starts at Zero
5. Simulation Uses the Same Rules
6. Concrete Before Abstract
7. Time Matters
8. Nothing Exists Alone
9. Graceful Unknowns

## Doctrine Statement

Velvet's intelligence is grounded not in isolated thoughts, but in a persistent, shared, evolving model of reality. Every organ observes the same world. Every decision changes that world. Every memory belongs to it.
