# Medical Guardian and Minimal-Risk Stop Lineage

Status: first-pass provenance reconstruction

Provenance state: `CONFIRMED_INTERNAL`, `CONVERGENT`

## Primary Origin

This architecture did not begin as an abstract autonomous-driving exercise.

Its primary recovered origin is a founder-confirmed real-world safety requirement: a driver may become suddenly unresponsive because of a neurological, circulatory, or other medical event, and the vehicle should support a bounded path toward a minimal-risk stop while preserving evidence and human authority.

That requirement shaped Temperance and Charlotte:

- Temperance observes medical and driver-state evidence
- Charlotte owns the driving-specialty proposal for a minimal-risk manoeuvre
- Runtime verifies current body and session context
- Court applies standing doctrine and policy
- approved executors act only through bounded paths
- Receipts preserve the decision, action, outcome, and uncertainty

## Recovered Scenario

The early scenario included:

- driver heartbeat or living-motion evidence
- lateral body shift
- inward camera evidence
- steering or brake inactivity
- future wearable input
- a short confirmation window
- driver-unresponsive audio
- hazards and a shoulder or minimal-risk stop
- optional horn burst
- stand-down of noncritical modules
- continued medical logging
- optional emergency call or message
- explicit “resume control” confirmation

A later second-vehicle research plan explored portability to an older automatic-transmission retrofit body through a theory and prototyping phase rather than immediate full installation. Personal family and medical details are intentionally excluded from the public provenance record.

## Internal Design Laws

- no single sensor may declare a medical emergency
- raw sensor data does not authorize takeover
- absence of response is evidence, not certainty
- emergency doctrine must be established in advance
- an emergency organ does not become the owner
- a minimal-risk stop is not ordinary autonomous driving
- physical action remains bounded by Runtime, Court, safety gates, and receipts
- uncertain outcomes must remain uncertain
- stand-down may suspend noncritical systems without destroying medical evidence
- recovery and resumption require explicit confirmation

## Convergent Engineering Families

The design is convergent with:

- driver monitoring systems
- driver incapacitation detection
- minimal-risk manoeuvre concepts
- advanced driver-assistance systems
- functional-safety hazard analysis
- fail-safe and fail-operational design
- medical alert fusion
- human confirmation and escalation protocols
- aviation-style incapacitation response concepts

These are comparison families. No named automotive standard, manufacturer system, paper, or regulatory framework is yet proven as the original source of the Velvet design.

## Adopt / Adapt / Reject Reconstruction

### Adopt

- multimodal corroboration
- short confirmation windows
- graded escalation
- minimum-risk objective
- explicit stand-down and recovery states
- continuous event and receipt logging
- separation of detection, decision, and actuation

### Adapt

- driver-monitoring concepts to retrofit vehicles with heterogeneous sensors
- minimal-risk manoeuvres to manual-transmission and mechanically actuated retrofit bodies
- medical alerting into a Unified-Organ chain between Temperance, Charlotte, Court, and Receipts
- emergency doctrine into owner-established standing authority rather than improvised model autonomy

### Reject

- one sensor triggering vehicle takeover
- generic “AI confidence” authorizing steering or braking
- hidden emergency modes
- medical diagnosis claims from vehicle sensors
- uncontrolled reinforcement learning on braking, steering, clutch, or throttle
- resuming control without verified context
- treating a successful simulation as proof of road safety

## Relationship to Unified-Organ AI

The medical scenario helped prove why Velvet could not be an agent swarm.

Temperance and Charlotte require different specialties, but one body must remain accountable. Their coordination is doctrine-delegated, not a negotiation between autonomous agents.

## Relationship to Doctrine of Silence

Ordinary notices may wait. Medical evidence cannot be permanently suppressed by quiet mode.

Even then, an attention interrupt is not the same as physical safeing authority.

## Relationship to Simulated Body

The medical and minimal-risk path requires extensive simulated-body testing before hardware-in-loop or road validation:

- sensor dropout
- contradictory evidence
- false-positive clusters
- delayed response
- actuator failure
- incomplete shoulder availability
- interrupted manoeuvres
- stale owner confirmation
- recovery after false escalation

Simulation must exercise the same Event Protocol, Court, execution, and receipt boundaries as live hardware.

## Current Attribution
